Habit Run Concept

Classic Habit Run Model

Habit Run is built around helping beginners build consistency through structured 5K community runs in Bengaluru. The classic format is guidance-led and beginner-first.

  • Monthly 5K, often on the last weekend.
  • Guided support ratio around 1 volunteer for 6-7 beginners.
  • More info: habit.run

Option 1

Run + Breakfast

Meet the group, run at a friendly pace, and then head to MTR with everyone.

  • Beginner-friendly social run.
  • No timing pressure.
  • Best way to meet fellow runners.

Option 2

Breakfast Only

Not running that day? No issue. Directly join us at MTR and be part of the meetup.

  • Open to everyone.
  • No signup or approval needed.
  • Pay only for your own order.
