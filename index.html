Habit Run Concept

The Original Format

From the Habit Run December 2025 listing: Habit Run was built to help beginners get into running, with a 5K run on the last weekend of every month and guided support.

  • Beginner-first 5K structure.
  • Guidance ratio of roughly 1 volunteer for 6-7 beginners.
  • 37 editions were already completed by December 2025.

New Social Format

What This Is (and Isn't)

  • This is a socializing meetup, not a competitive fitness club.
  • You can run, walk, or directly join us at breakfast.
  • No gimmicks, no labels, no commitment required.
  • More about Habit Run: habit.run

Morning Plan

Show up at 7:30 AM. Meet people. Have breakfast. Head back.

Start near Karachi Bakery, move for a short walk-run loop, then regroup at the iconic MTR Indiranagar. You pay for what you eat. That's it.
